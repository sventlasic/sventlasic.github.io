Skybite

Six fast arcade scenes for Android. One rule: survive as long as you can. Every run gets faster until it beats you, and the next one starts with a single tap.

Get it on Google Play

The scenes

  • Survival — hold your row and pop the balloons off falling animals before they land.
  • Balloon Climb — steer a bunch of balloons upward through animals, coconuts and storm clouds.
  • Monkey Memory — remember who hides under the drifting leaves, then find the one the monkey calls.
  • Sky Bridge — keep the falling animals from breaking the planks under your feet.
  • Monkey Whack — whack only what the monkey's sign shows, and never what it forbids.
  • Shrinking Land — twin-stick survival on a square of land that loses a ring every minute.

Plus Versus for two phones on the same Wi-Fi.

Good to know

  • Free to play, supported by a banner ad. Three scenes unlock by watching one short video ad, chosen by you.
  • No account and no sign-in. Your scores and settings stay on your phone.
  • Works offline, apart from the ads and Versus.
