Skybite: Tester Guide

Everything you need to know to test the game. Reading time: about 5 minutes.

Link to this guide: https://sventlasic.github.io/skybite/tester-guide.html

Thank you for helping! Skybite is a free Android arcade game: six short survival scenes, each getting harder until it beats you. No account, no sign-up, and everything except the ads and the two-phone mode works without the internet.

Before the game can go public on Google Play, Google requires at least 12 testers to stay in the test for 14 days in a row. That is the main thing we need from you, plus anything wrong you notice along the way.

1. What we need from you

TimeAbout 20 minutes to try every scene, then play now and then for 14 days
You needAn Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer) and a Google account
Please don'tUninstall the game or leave the test before day 14. If we drop below 12 testers, the 14 days start over for everyone
BonusTell us what is too hard, too easy, confusing, or broken, and how long you lasted

2. How to join (do this once)

  1. Send us the Google email you use in the Play Store on your phone. The invite only works for that address.
  2. Open the tester link after we tell you that you have been added: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.sventlasic.skybite
  3. Tap Become a tester. The page then says you are a tester. This step is what Google counts.
  4. Tap download it on Google Play on that page, and install the game.

Install from Google Play only. A game file sent by chat does not count toward the 12 testers.

It can take a few hours after joining before the game appears in the Play Store. If it says "item not found", wait a bit and open the link again.

Testing Civil Service Reviewer Today too? It has its own tester link. Join both, one link per app.

3. First run

  1. The home page shows SINGLE PLAYER, VERSUS BATTLE (LAN) and SETTINGS. There is no sign-in.
  2. SINGLE PLAYER opens the SCENES list: six games. The first three are open; the last three say LOCKED (see section 5).
  3. The first time you play a scene, it teaches itself with a few short prompts over the real game. You can tap SKIP. Each scene has its own tutorial switch on the SCENES list, if you want a lesson again.
  4. If you are in Europe or the UK, Google may ask about ad consent. Either answer is fine; the game works the same.

5. Things that are supposed to be like this

Not bugs, no need to report them:

  • The ads are test ads. During testing, banners and videos say "Test Ad". Tapping them is safe: they earn and cost nothing.
  • Sometimes the banner is one of our own apps, marked OUR APP, instead of an ad. That is our own promotion, shown when Google has no ad.
  • Three scenes are locked. Sky Bridge, Monkey Whack and Balloon Climb each unlock for good by watching one short video ad to the end. Closing it early unlocks nothing. If no ad loads, it says so, and after three tries the scene opens free.
  • Every scene gets very hard. That is the design: each one keeps speeding up until it wins. Surviving a long time is meant to be rare.
  • In Survival, the Back button does nothing during a run, so an accidental swipe cannot throw your run away. In the other scenes, Back asks first: press it twice to leave.
  • In Monkey Memory and Monkey Whack the gear does not pause. The clock is the challenge there; the settings screen covers the board instead and says the game is still running.
  • Your bests stay on this phone. There are no online leaderboards.
  • Versus may not find the other phone on some home networks (two routers, or "guest" Wi-Fi). Use TYPE AN ADDRESS on the joining phone, or put both phones on the same router.
  • iPhone: there is no iPhone version.

6. Your privacy

  • There is no account. We don't know your name or email from the game.
  • Your scores, settings and unlocked scenes are stored only on your phone. Nothing about how you play is sent anywhere.
  • The ads come from Google AdMob, which receives the information described in our Privacy Policy: https://sventlasic.github.io/skybite/privacy.html
  • SHARE only hands a line of text to your phone's share menu. You choose where it goes; the game sends nothing itself.

7. Found a problem? Tell us

Email nevssupplies+skybite@gmail.com, or message Sven directly, whichever is easier.

These help us fix it faster:

  1. Your phone (for example Redmi Note 13) and Android version (Settings → About phone)
  2. The game version, shown at the bottom of Skybite's SETTINGS on the home page
  3. Which scene, and roughly how long into the run it happened
  4. What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  5. A screenshot or screen recording, if you can. This helps the most.

Feel matters as much as bugs: a button that is hard to hit, a thumb that keeps covering something, a scene that is unfair rather than hard, a moment that is boring. Honest reactions are worth more than polite ones.

8. If something goes wrong

ProblemTry this
"Item not found" in the Play StoreYou haven't tapped Become a tester yet, or you joined with a different Google account than the one on your phone. Open the link again and check the account.
The game won't installAn older copy may still be on the phone, also in Second space, Dual apps or a work profile on Xiaomi, Samsung and similar phones. Uninstall it everywhere, then install from the Play Store.
A locked scene says "no ad right now"Check your internet and try again. After three tries the scene opens free.
No soundCheck the gear (or home SETTINGS): Background music and Sound effects are separate switches. Also check the phone's media volume.
The Play Store offers an updatePlease update. We send fixes during the test.

9. The 14 days

DayWhat happens
Day 0You tap Become a tester and install the game
Days 1 to 13Keep it installed. Play now and then, for example a few runs every couple of days
Day 14We apply to Google for the public launch

The one thing that breaks this is leaving the test or uninstalling early. If the count drops below 12, the 14 days restart for everyone. If you need to leave, please tell us first so we can find someone else.

The full list of what the game is supposed to do is here, if you want to check something against it: https://sventlasic.github.io/skybite/frs.html
