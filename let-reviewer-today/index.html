LET Reviewer Today

A free reviewer app for people preparing for the Licensure Examination for Professional Teachers (LET) in the Philippines, Elementary and Secondary levels.

  • Free, with no account and no paid version
  • General Education, Professional Education, and the English and Mathematics majors
  • Practice questions with the answer explained after each one, and short lessons for every topic
  • Timed mock exams that follow the official table of specifications
  • Works without an internet connection

Get it on Google Play
