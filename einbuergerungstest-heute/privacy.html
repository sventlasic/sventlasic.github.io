Privacy Policy — Einbürgerungstest Heute

Last updated: November 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who we are

Einbürgerungstest Heute (the "App") is a free study aid for the German naturalisation test and the "Leben in Deutschland" test. It is operated by Laurel Lasic, an individual developer based in the Philippines ("we"), who is the controller responsible for the processing described in this policy.

For any privacy question, contact us at nevssupplies+einbuergerungstest@gmail.com.

The App is an unofficial study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the BAMF or any government body.

This policy is available in English and German. Both versions say the same thing.

2. The short version

  • The App has no accounts and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any ID.
  • Your study progress and the federal state you choose are saved only on your phone. We cannot see them.
  • The App shows advertisements from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to deliver those ads, as explained in section 5.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store on our own servers any of the following:

  • your name, email address, phone number or home address
  • your nationality, residence status, or anything about your naturalisation or integration course
  • the federal state you choose in the App
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone
  • your answers, scores or study history

If you email us. Settings → Send feedback and "Report a problem with this question" open your own email app with a draft to us. Nothing is sent unless you send it. The draft includes the App version, your Android version and phone model, and for a report the question number, which help us fix problems; you can delete any of it before sending. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote, use them only to reply and to fix the App, and delete the email when it is no longer needed. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you and improving the App (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Information stored on your phone

To work, the App saves the following on your device only:

  • which questions you have answered and whether you got them right
  • your bookmarks, practice history, study streak and mock test results
  • your settings, such as your federal state, interface language, daily goal and study reminder time
  • downloaded copies of the question file and the guides

This information never leaves your phone. We have no copy of it, which also means we cannot restore it if it is lost.

You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset my progress, by clearing the App's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the App.

5. Advertising

The App is funded by banner advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location such as city or country
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • how the App and its ads are used, such as app launches, which ads were shown and whether they were tapped
  • performance information about the App and the ad software, such as how long the App takes to start

Google is responsible for how it handles this data. Google may also process data in countries outside the European Union, such as the United States, on the basis of the safeguards it describes. See Google's explanation at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and its privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Legal basis. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads and the storing and reading of information on your device for advertising are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25 of the German TDDDG). Ads that are not personalised are shown on the basis of our legitimate interest in funding the free App (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ads are loaded. If you do not consent, you may still see ads that are not personalised. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

We do not give advertisers any information about your studying or your federal state, because we do not have it.

Our own banners. When no advertisement is available, the App may show a banner for one of our other apps. The list of these banners and their icons is downloaded from our website, as described in section 6, and they collect nothing. Tapping one opens the Google Play Store, which is governed by Google's own policies.

6. Question, guide and banner updates

From time to time the App downloads updated question and guide files, and the list of our own app banners with their icons, from our website (sventlasic.github.io, hosted by GitHub). This lets you get corrected answers and a new catalogue without installing a new version. Like any website request, these downloads reveal your IP address to our hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs. They do not include any information about you, your federal state or your progress. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the App's content correct (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

7. Study reminders

If you turn on a study reminder, the App asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. We do not send them from a server and we do not collect a notification token. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

8. Sharing

We do not sell personal data, and we have none to sell. The only parties that receive data in connection with the App are:

  • Google AdMob, for advertising, as described in section 5
  • our website hosting provider, for the downloads described in section 6
  • authorities, if we are ever required by law, to the extent we hold anything relevant

When you share your score or the App, your phone's own share menu is used and we receive nothing. If the App invites you to rate it, the rating is handled by Google Play.

9. How long data is kept

Information on your phone stays there until you reset it, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. We hold no personal data about you on our side, apart from an email you choose to send us (section 3). Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policies.

10. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the rights given by the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection: to access, correct and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. You have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live. In Germany, these are the data protection authorities of the federal states.

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you also have the right to be informed, to access, to correct and to erase your personal data, to object to its processing, and to data portability, and you may file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at https://privacy.gov.ph.

Because your study data exists only on your phone, you exercise most of these rights yourself, directly in the App or in your phone's settings. For anything else, contact nevssupplies+einbuergerungstest@gmail.com.

11. Children

The App is intended for adults preparing for the naturalisation test or the integration course test. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add a new feature or service. The current version and its date are always available in the App and on our website. If a change affects what data is collected, we will say so in the App.
