2. What the App is

The App is a free study aid for the German naturalisation test (Einbürgerungstest) and the "Leben in Deutschland" test taken at the end of the integration course. It offers the official catalogue questions with explanations, practice by topic and by federal state, timed mock tests, and progress tracking.
