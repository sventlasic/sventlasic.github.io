Reviewer Today: EPSO Test Prep

A free Android app for practising the verbal, numerical and abstract reasoning tests used in selection procedures for the EU institutions.

  • Free, with no account and no paid version
  • Practice questions with the answer explained after each one
  • Timed mock exams, run as separate tests like the real ones
  • Short guides for each type of test
  • Works without an internet connection

Unofficial study app. Not affiliated with or endorsed by EPSO, the European Union or any EU institution. Test formats change from one selection procedure to another. Official information, including notices of competition, comes only from EPSO at https://eu-careers.europa.eu.
