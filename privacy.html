Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Version 1.1

1. Who we are

HelperHive ("we") is a Philippines-focused marketplace app connecting employers with household helpers. For privacy matters, contact: helperhivesupport@gmail.com

2. What we collect

We deliberately collect as little as possible:

  • From your Google or Facebook sign-in: your name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Your chosen role (employer or helper) and, for helpers, the profile details you enter: skills, rate, work schedule, and a short bio.
  • Your city-level location only — either detected once from GPS or picked manually. GPS coordinates are used in memory only to determine your city name and are never stored. We never collect or store your street address, barangay, ZIP code, phone number, or government IDs.
  • Content you create in the App: messages, reviews, and reports.
  • A device token for push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • App usage and interaction data — which screens you view and features you use — collected through Google Analytics for Firebase and linked to an app-generated identifier. This helps us understand how the App is used. It does not include your name, email, or precise location.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — error reports, plus your device model and operating-system version — collected through Firebase Crashlytics if the App crashes, so we can diagnose and fix the problem.

3. How we use your data

To operate the App: showing your profile to potential matches in your city, enabling chat, delivering notifications, displaying reviews, and keeping the community safe (report handling). We also use anonymous usage and crash data to understand how the App is used and to diagnose and fix problems, so we can improve it. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

4. Sign-in providers

When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive only your name, email, and photo from them. We never see your passwords. Google's and Meta's own privacy policies govern their handling of your login.

5. Advertising

The App shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show relevant ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android). We do not share your HelperHive profile data with advertisers.

6. Where your data lives

Your data is stored in and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, and Crashlytics), encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by security rules so that, for example, only chat participants can read their messages.

7. Sharing

We share data only with: (a) other users, as part of how the App works — your profile is visible to signed-in users, and your messages to their recipients; (b) our service providers Google Firebase and Google AdMob, as described above; and (c) authorities, if required by law.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your data only while your account exists. You can permanently delete your account at any time from Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Account, or by emailing helperhivesupport@gmail.com. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone: we erase your profile and your sign-in account — your name, email, profile photo, and city — so we can no longer identify you. Conversations you took part in remain in the other person's chat history but appear as "Deleted user," and any job posts you created are removed. Some content you created — reviews you wrote, and notifications generated by your activity — may be retained, but it keeps only your display name, never your email or photo. We may also retain limited information where the law requires it or to prevent fraud and abuse.

9. Your rights (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, RA 10173)

You have the right to be informed, to access, correct, and delete your personal data, to object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact helperhivesupport@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

10. Children

HelperHive is for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from minors; such accounts will be deleted.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version and date are always shown in the App and on our website. Material changes will be flagged in the App.
