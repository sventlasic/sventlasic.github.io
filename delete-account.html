Delete your HelperHive account

HelperHive · published by Lasic Apps

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Once you confirm, your account and profile are erased straight away — there is no recovery period and no way to restore them.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app fastest

  1. Open the HelperHive app and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab (bottom right).
  3. Tap Privacy & Security.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm when asked. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it

If you can no longer sign in, email helperhivesupport@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject “Delete my account”.

We verify that the request comes from the account owner, then delete the account. We aim to complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

All of the following are erased permanently, so we can no longer identify you:

  • Your name, email address and profile photo
  • Your city and your role (employer or helper)
  • Your helper profile — skills, daily rate, availability, schedule and bio
  • Any job posts you created, and applications you sent
  • Your notification device tokens
  • Your sign-in account itself

What is kept, and why

  • Conversations you took part in remain in the other person's chat history, because they are that person's messages too. You appear only as “Deleted user”.
  • Reviews you wrote about another user are retained so that person's rating stays accurate. They keep only your display name — never your email or photo.
  • Notifications generated by your past activity may remain for the recipient, again showing only a display name.
  • We may retain limited records where the law requires it, or to prevent fraud and abuse.

Retention period

There is no additional retention period for the data listed under “What is deleted” — it is removed at the moment you confirm. The limited items listed under “What is kept” are retained for as long as the other user's account or our legal obligations require.
