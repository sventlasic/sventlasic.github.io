1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This document lists what the app is required to do, so that testers can check its behaviour against a written baseline. Every requirement has a unique ID; quote it when reporting a finding (for example "FR-MOCK-07 fails: the timer kept running after I left").

1.2 Scope

Civil Service Reviewer Today is a free Android study app for the Philippine Civil Service Examination, Professional and Sub-Professional levels. It offers practice questions with explanations, timed mock exams, short lessons ("reviewers") and progress tracking. There is no account, no paid tier and no backend: study data stays on the device, and the app is funded by banner ads.

1.3 Intended audience

Testers, the developer, and anyone checking that the app is complete. Section 3 is the part to test; Sections 2 and 4 give the context needed to judge whether behaviour is correct.

1.4 Definitions

TermMeaning
LevelThe exam being prepared for: Professional or Sub-Professional
SubjectVerbal Ability, Numerical Ability, Analytical Ability (Professional), Clerical Ability (Sub-Professional), General Information
Practice setA quiz of up to 20 questions, answered one at a time with immediate feedback
Mock examA timed exam with feedback only after submitting: Full or Quick
PassageA reading text with several questions; its questions always appear together and in order
Reviewer / lessonA short study note for one topic, with a "Practice this topic" button
BankAll questions available to the app: those built into it plus those downloaded from the website
House bannerA banner for one of our own apps, shown in an ad slot when no ad is available

1.5 Requirement conventions

  • Shall marks a requirement. Each is written so that it can be passed or failed by observation.
  • Priority: M must (blocks launch), S should, C could.
  • IDs are stable. A withdrawn requirement keeps its ID retired, so a gap in numbering is not a finding.

2. Overall description

2.1 Operating environment

  • Android 7.0 or newer, phones and tablets, portrait orientation.
  • Distributed through Google Play. Closed-testing builds show Google test ads only.
  • An internet connection is needed only for ads and for downloading new questions and lessons; everything else works offline.

2.2 Constraints

#Constraint
C-1No account or sign-in of any kind.
C-2Answers, scores, bookmarks and settings are stored only on the device and never sent anywhere.
C-3No iOS version.
C-4The app is an independent study aid, not affiliated with the Civil Service Commission, and says so.
C-5A question's ID never changes and is never reused, because progress is stored against it.

3. Functional requirements

3.1 First run and navigation: FR-NAV

IDRequirementPriority
FR-NAV-01On first launch the app shall show a welcome screen describing the app and asking which exam the user is taking (Professional selected by default).M
FR-NAV-02The welcome screen shall link to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy and state that the app is not affiliated with the Civil Service Commission.M
FR-NAV-03Tapping Start reviewing shall save the chosen level and open Home; the welcome screen shall not appear again on later launches.M
FR-NAV-04The app shall have four tabs: Home, Reviewers, Practice, Progress.M
FR-NAV-05On Home (and on the welcome screen), pressing Back once shall show "Press back again to exit"; a second press within 2 seconds shall close the app.S

3.2 Home: FR-HOME

IDRequirementPriority
FR-HOME-01Home shall show the current level, which can be changed from the level button; changing level shall keep all progress.M
FR-HOME-02Home shall show today's goal as "X of Y questions" with a progress bar, and the current day streak (or "No streak yet").M
FR-HOME-03The goal card button shall read Start practice before any answer today and Continue practice afterwards, and start a mixed practice set.M
FR-HOME-04Home shall show a Start mock exam button and one card per subject of the current level with its "% done".M
FR-HOME-05"% done" shall be the share of that subject's questions answered at least once at the current level.S

3.3 Practice: FR-PRAC

IDRequirementPriority
FR-PRAC-01A practice set shall contain up to 20 questions of the current level, mixed or from one chosen subject or lesson topic.M
FR-PRAC-02Questions shall be chosen in this priority: never answered, then answered wrong last time, then answered longest ago. The chosen questions are shuffled.M
FR-PRAC-03The four choices shall be shown in a new random order each time a question appears.M
FR-PRAC-04After the user taps a choice, the app shall mark the user's choice and the correct answer, show "Correct" or "Not quite" with the explanation, and not allow changing the answer.M
FR-PRAC-05Under every explanation there shall be Report a problem with this question, which opens an email to the support address with the question ID filled in.M
FR-PRAC-06Passage questions shall show the passage text and appear together, in order.M
FR-PRAC-07The user shall be able to bookmark and un-bookmark the current question.S
FR-PRAC-08Closing a set early shall ask for confirmation; answers given so far shall be kept.M
FR-PRAC-09After the last question the app shall show the score ("X of 20").M
FR-PRAC-10Every answer shall count toward today's goal, the streak and the statistics.M

3.4 Mock exam: FR-MOCK

IDRequirementPriority
FR-MOCK-01The mock exam intro shall offer a Full and a Quick exam with the question count and time limit of the current level (see 2.3), and explain the rules.M
FR-MOCK-02The exam shall cover all subjects of the level in roughly equal shares, grouped by subject, with passages kept together.M
FR-MOCK-03The exam shall show a countdown timer that changes appearance when little time is left.M
FR-MOCK-04The user shall be able to move with Previous and Next, flag questions, and jump to any question from All questions.M
FR-MOCK-05No answer or explanation shall be shown before the exam is submitted.M
FR-MOCK-06Submitting shall ask for confirmation and say how many questions are unanswered; unanswered questions count as wrong.M
FR-MOCK-07When the user leaves the exam or the app goes to the background, the timer shall pause and the exam shall be saved.M
FR-MOCK-08A saved exam shall be offered on the intro screen as "Resume exam", naming the level, the question reached and the time left.M
FR-MOCK-09When the timer reaches zero the exam shall be submitted automatically.M
FR-MOCK-10If the bank has fewer questions than the full format, the exam shall be shortened and the time scaled to match, and the intro shall say so.S

3.5 Results and review: FR-RES

IDRequirementPriority
FR-RES-01After a mock exam the app shall show the total score, the percentage, whether it reaches the 80% passing mark, and the score per subject.M
FR-RES-02Review my mistakes shall list each wrong or unanswered question with the user's answer, the correct answer and the explanation.M
FR-RES-03The results screen shall offer to take a new mock exam, to practise the missed questions, and to go back home.S
FR-RES-04Every submitted exam shall appear in Progress → Mock exam history with its date and score, and can be reopened.M
FR-RES-05The user shall be able to share a score through the phone's share menu; nothing is sent by the app itself.C

3.6 Reviewers (lessons): FR-LES

IDRequirementPriority
FR-LES-01The Reviewers tab shall list the lessons of the current level, filterable by subject.M
FR-LES-02A lesson shall show its subject, its position ("Lesson 1 of 8"), and sections such as Key idea, Worked example, Remember and Common mistake.M
FR-LES-03Practice this topic shall start a practice set from that lesson's topic.M

3.7 Progress: FR-PROG

IDRequirementPriority
FR-PROG-01Progress shall show the day streak, the overall accuracy and "X of Y answered" for the current level.M
FR-PROG-02Progress shall show accuracy per subject ("Not started" when none answered).M
FR-PROG-03Progress shall suggest a Focus next topic: the weakest topic with enough answers, never a topic answered perfectly.S
FR-PROG-04Progress shall open Mock exam history, Bookmarked questions and Questions I got wrong, each with a count.M
FR-PROG-05A day counts toward the streak if at least one question was answered that day; the streak does not break until a whole day passes without answers.M

3.8 Settings and reminders: FR-SET

IDRequirementPriority
FR-SET-01Settings shall allow changing the exam level and the daily goal (10, 20, 30 or 50 questions; 20 by default).M
FR-SET-02The user shall be able to turn on one daily study reminder and choose its time.M
FR-SET-03When on, a notification shall appear at the chosen time each day; tapping it opens the app.M
FR-SET-04On phones whose maker restricts background work (for example Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO), Settings shall explain how to let reminders through and offer Open App settings.S
FR-SET-05Settings shall offer Rate this app, Share with a friend, Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.S
FR-SET-06Reset my progress shall ask for confirmation and then erase answers, bookmarks, the streak and the exam history, keeping settings such as the level.M
FR-SET-07Settings shall show the app version and state that progress is stored only on this phone.C

3.9 Questions and lessons from the website: FR-CONT

IDRequirementPriority
FR-CONT-01The app shall work fully offline with its built-in questions and lessons.M
FR-CONT-02When online, the app shall check the website for a newer question file and lesson file, download them in the background, and use them from the next launch.M
FR-CONT-03A failed or interrupted download shall leave the current questions in use, with no error shown to the user.M
FR-CONT-04Progress on existing questions shall be kept after an update.M

3.10 Ads, consent and promotions: FR-ADS

IDRequirementPriority
FR-ADS-01Tab screens shall show a banner at the bottom, above the tab bar; other screens at the top, with the space reserved so the screen does not jump when the ad loads.M
FR-ADS-02Closed-testing builds shall show only Google test ads, labelled "Test Ad".M
FR-ADS-03Before any ad is requested, the app shall run Google's consent check; users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see a consent form on first launch, with Consent, Do not consent and Manage options.M
FR-ADS-04Refusing consent shall not block any feature.M
FR-ADS-05Users who saw a consent form (EEA, UK, Switzerland, regulated US states) shall have an Ad privacy choices row in Settings that reopens it.M
FR-ADS-06When no ad can be shown, the slot shall show a banner for one of our other apps, marked "OUR APP", and never for this app itself.S

3.11 Rating: FR-RATE

IDRequirementPriority
FR-RATE-01The app shall ask for a Play Store rating, using Google's own rating window, after the 3rd finished practice set or straight after passing a mock exam.S
FR-RATE-02The app shall not ask again within 60 days, shall offer no reward for rating, and shall not ask "Do you like the app?" first.M

4. Non-functional requirements

IDRequirementPriority
NFR-01No crash or freeze during normal use on Android 7.0 or newer.M
NFR-02All text readable and nothing cut off on small phones and on tablets.M
NFR-03Opening the app, a practice set or a lesson shall feel immediate; no screen waits on the network.S
NFR-04Nothing about the user leaves the device apart from what the ads SDK sends (see the Privacy Policy).M
NFR-05Every question has exactly one correct answer, and its explanation agrees with it.M

5. Out of scope for version 1

  • Accounts, sync between devices, and leaderboards.
  • An iPhone version.
  • A landscape layout (Android 16 and newer tablets may still rotate the app).
  • Paid features or ad removal.
