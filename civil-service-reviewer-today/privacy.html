Privacy Policy — Civil Service Reviewer Today

Last updated: October 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who we are

Civil Service Reviewer Today (the "App") is a free reviewer for people preparing for the Philippine Civil Service Examination. It is operated by Laurel Lasic, an individual developer based in the Philippines ("we").

For any privacy question, contact us at nevssupplies+civilservice@gmail.com.

The App is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Civil Service Commission or any other government agency.

2. The short version

  • The App has no accounts and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any ID.
  • Your study progress is saved only on your phone. We cannot see it.
  • The App shows advertisements from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to deliver those ads, as explained in section 5.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, or store on our own servers any of the following:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or home address
  • government IDs or exam application details
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone
  • your answers, scores, or study history

4. Information stored on your phone

To work, the App saves the following on your device only:

  • the exam level you chose (Professional or Sub-Professional)
  • which questions you have answered and whether you got them right
  • your bookmarks, practice scores, mock exam history, and study streak
  • your settings, such as your daily goal and study reminder time
  • a downloaded copy of the question bank

This information never leaves your phone. We have no copy of it, which also means we cannot restore it if it is lost.

You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset my progress, by clearing the App's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the App.

5. Advertising

The App is funded by banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location such as city or country
  • device information such as model, operating system version, and language
  • how the App and its ads are used, such as app launches, which ads were shown and whether they were tapped
  • performance information about the App and the ad software, such as how long the App takes to start

Google is responsible for how it handles this data. See Google's explanation at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and its privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ads are loaded. If you do not consent, you may still see ads that are not personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalization in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

We do not give advertisers any information about your studying, because we do not have it.

Our own banners. When no advertisement is available, the App may show a banner for one of our other apps. The list of these banners and their icons is downloaded from our website, as described in section 6, and they collect nothing. Tapping one opens the Google Play Store, which is governed by Google's own policies.

6. Question, lesson and banner updates

From time to time the App downloads updated question and lesson files, and the list of our own app banners with their icons, from our website. This lets you get new and corrected questions and lessons without installing a new version. Like any website request, these downloads reveal your IP address to our hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs. They do not include any information about you or your progress.

7. Study reminders

If you turn on a study reminder, the App asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. We do not send them from a server and we do not collect a notification token. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

8. Sharing

We do not sell personal data, and we have none to sell. The only parties that receive data in connection with the App are:

  • Google AdMob, for advertising, as described in section 5
  • our website hosting provider, for the downloads described in section 6
  • authorities, if we are ever required by law, to the extent we hold anything relevant

9. How long data is kept

Information on your phone stays there until you reset it, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. We hold no personal data about you on our side. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policies.

10. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, and to erase your personal data, to object to its processing, and to data portability.

Because your study data exists only on your phone, you exercise most of these rights yourself, directly in the App or in your phone's settings. For anything else, contact nevssupplies+civilservice@gmail.com. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at https://privacy.gov.ph.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, including the right to withdraw your advertising consent at any time and the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

The App is intended for people preparing for the Civil Service Examination, who must be at least 18 years old to apply. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add a new feature or service. The current version and its date are always available in the App and on our website. If a change affects what data is collected, we will say so in the App.
