Civil Service Reviewer Today: Tester Guide

Everything you need to know to test the app. Reading time: about 5 minutes.

Link to this guide: https://sventlasic.github.io/civil-service-reviewer-today/tester-guide.html

Thank you for helping! Civil Service Reviewer Today is a free Android app for people preparing for the Philippine Civil Service Exam, Professional and Sub-Professional. It has practice questions with explanations, timed mock exams and short lessons. No account, no sign-up, and it works without mobile data.

Before the app can go public on Google Play, Google requires at least 12 testers to stay in the test for 14 days in a row. That is the main thing we need from you, plus anything wrong you notice along the way.

1. What we need from you

TimeAbout 15 minutes to try it, then use it now and then for 14 days
You needAn Android phone or tablet (Android 7.0 or newer) and a Google account
Please don'tUninstall the app or leave the test before day 14. If we drop below 12 testers, the 14 days start over for everyone
BonusTell us about wrong answers, confusing questions, typos, or anything that feels broken or slow

2. How to join (do this once)

  1. Send us the Google email you use in the Play Store on your phone. The invite only works for that address.
  2. Open the tester link after we tell you that you have been added: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.civilservicereviewertoday.app
  3. Tap Become a tester. The page then says you are a tester. This step is what Google counts.
  4. Tap download it on Google Play on that page, and install the app.

Install from Google Play only. An app file sent by chat does not count toward the 12 testers.

It can take a few hours after joining before the app appears in the Play Store. If it says "item not found", wait a bit and open the link again.

Testing Skybite too? Skybite has its own tester link. Join both, one link per app.

3. First run

  1. A welcome screen shows what the app does.
  2. Choose your exam: Professional or Sub-Professional. You can change it later in Settings, and your progress is kept.
  3. Tap Start reviewing. That is all: no sign-in.
  4. If you are in Europe or the UK, Google may ask about ad consent. Either answer is fine; the app works the same.

4. What to try

You don't have to do everything, but the more you cover, the more useful your help is. Roughly in order of importance:

Practice

  • Tap Start practice on Home and answer a full set of 20 questions to the end
  • After each answer, check that the correct answer and the explanation make sense
  • Practice one subject from Home or the Practice tab
  • Bookmark a question (the bookmark icon at the top right), then find it under Progress → Bookmarked questions
  • Get a few wrong on purpose, then open Progress → Questions I got wrong

Mock exam

  • Start a Quick exam (50 questions, 55 minutes)
  • Use Previous / Next, flag a question, and open All questions
  • Leave in the middle and come back later: your place and the timer should be saved
  • Submit, then check your score, the score per subject, and the review of your answers

Reviewers (lessons)

  • Open the Reviewers tab, read a lesson, and tap Practice this topic

Settings and the rest

  • Change the daily goal, and turn on the study reminder at a time a few minutes away. Did the notification come?
  • Switch the exam level and back
  • Try the app with mobile data and Wi-Fi turned off. It should still work
  • Tap Report a problem with this question under an explanation (it opens an email with the question number filled in)

Checking the questions
We may also send you a spreadsheet with every question, its choices and the answer. If you have time, mark each one OK or Problem and write what is wrong. Even 20 checked questions help.

5. Things that are supposed to be like this

Not bugs, no need to report them:

  • The ads are test ads. During testing, banners say "Test Ad". Tapping them is safe: they earn and cost nothing.
  • Sometimes the banner is "HelperHive, OUR APP" (or another of our apps) instead of an ad. That is our own promotion, shown when Google has no ad.
  • The answer letters change. The order of the choices is shuffled every time, so the same question can have its answer at B one day and D the next.
  • New questions and lessons arrive quietly. The app checks our website for new questions and uses them from the next time you open it. There is no "update" message.
  • A rating window may appear after a few practice sets. Rate honestly, or tap "Not now". During testing, your rating is only visible to us.
  • On Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones, reminders may only appear after you unlock the phone. Settings in the app explains how to fix it (Battery saver: No restrictions).
  • iPhone: there is no iPhone version.

6. Your privacy

  • There is no account. We don't know your name or email from the app.
  • Your answers, scores, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your phone. Nothing about your studying is sent anywhere.
  • The ads come from Google AdMob, which receives the information described in our Privacy Policy: https://sventlasic.github.io/civil-service-reviewer-today/privacy.html
  • Settings → Reset my progress erases everything the app saved. Please don't uninstall before day 14, though.

7. Found a problem? Tell us

Email nevssupplies+civilservice@gmail.com, or message Sven directly, whichever is easier.

For a wrong or confusing question, the fastest way is Report a problem with this question under the explanation: it fills in the question number for you.

For anything else, these help us fix it faster:

  1. Your phone (for example Redmi Note 13) and Android version (Settings → About phone)
  2. What you did, step by step, before it went wrong
  3. What you expected, and what happened instead
  4. A screenshot or screen recording, if you can. This helps the most.

Small things count too: a confusing word, a button that is hard to reach, text that is too small. Honest reactions are worth more than polite ones.

8. If something goes wrong

ProblemTry this
"Item not found" in the Play StoreYou haven't tapped Become a tester yet, or you joined with a different Google account than the one on your phone. Open the link again and check the account.
The app won't installAn older copy may still be on the phone, also in Second space, Dual apps or a work profile on Xiaomi, Samsung and similar phones. Uninstall it everywhere, then install from the Play Store.
No reminder notificationSettings in the app → turn on Study reminder. Then phone Settings → Apps → Civil Service Reviewer Today → allow notifications, and set Battery saver to No restrictions.
The Play Store offers an updatePlease update. We may send fixes during the test.

9. The 14 days

DayWhat happens
Day 0You tap Become a tester and install the app
Days 1 to 13Keep it installed. Open it now and then, for example a practice set every few days
Day 14We apply to Google for the public launch

The one thing that breaks this is leaving the test or uninstalling early. If the count drops below 12, the 14 days restart for everyone. If you need to leave, please tell us first so we can find someone else.
