1

Scope

HelperHive is an Android marketplace for the Philippines connecting employers who need household help with helpers who offer that work. It provides discovery, a job board, direct messaging, reviews, and safety tooling. It is funded by advertising — no fees, commission, or payments are handled in the app.

Roles

RoleSeesProfile
EmployerHelper listingsName + city (1 step)
HelperEmployer listingsPhoto, city, skills, schedule, bio (5 steps)
AdministratorModeration screensServer-assigned; not a public profile

Constraints

IDConstraint
C-1Google Sign-In is the only sign-in method exposed. Facebook is implemented but hidden behind a feature flag.
C-2No iOS build in V1.
C-3Profile photos stored inline as compressed base64 on the user record; Firebase Storage is not used.
C-4Chat is text only. Image messages are not sent.
C-5Role cannot be switched after sign-up.
C-6Location is city-level only; GPS coordinates are never persisted.
C-7All list queries are paginated; nothing fetches a whole collection.
Reading the priorities

Must = release-blocking for V1. Should and Could may ship incomplete without blocking release, but still report them when they fail.

2.1

Startup and availability gate

Prefix FR-GATE — what happens before the app is usable.

FR-GATE-01

On every app open the system shall evaluate availability in this order: maintenance first, then version.

Must
FR-GATE-02

When maintenance mode is on, the system shall display a non-dismissible Maintenance screen in place of the entire app, showing the configured message and estimated time.

Must
FR-GATE-03

When the installed version is below the minimum and force-update is on, the system shall display a non-dismissible Force Update screen with a link to the Play Store listing.

Must
FR-GATE-04

When the version is outdated but force-update is off, the system shall display a dismissible Optional Update prompt offering “Maybe Later”.

Must
FR-GATE-05

Gate configuration shall be read through a live listener, so changes reach already-running clients without a restart.

Should
FR-GATE-06

The gate shall fail open: if the configuration is missing or the listener errors, the user is admitted rather than locked out.

Must
FR-GATE-07

Version thresholds shall never be hardcoded in the client.

Must

2.3

Authentication and consent

Prefix FR-AUTH

FR-AUTH-01

The system shall allow sign-in with a Google account.

Must
FR-AUTH-02

The system shall not display a Facebook sign-in control while the Facebook feature flag is disabled.

Must
FR-AUTH-03

The system shall require acceptance of the Terms and Privacy Policy before sign-in completes; a user who does not accept shall not reach the application.

Must
FR-AUTH-04

Legal text shall be fetched from the remote configuration at display time, so it can be updated without an app release.

Must
FR-AUTH-05

On acceptance the system shall record the acceptance timestamp on the user record.

Must
FR-AUTH-06

After first sign-in the system shall require the user to select exactly one role: Employer or Helper.

Must
FR-AUTH-07

The selected role shall persist and shall not be changeable within V1.

Must
FR-AUTH-08

Returning users with a completed profile shall go directly to Home, bypassing onboarding, terms, role, and setup.

Must
FR-AUTH-09

The system shall present a human-readable message for each sign-in failure mode (cancelled, network, account collision) rather than a raw error.

Should
FR-AUTH-10

The system shall provide sign-out, which clears the session and de-registers the device push token.

Must
2.4

Profile setup

Prefix FR-PROF

FR-PROF-01

Helpers shall complete a five-step profile: photo, location, skills, schedule, bio.

Must
FR-PROF-02

Employers shall complete a one-step profile: name and location.

Must
FR-PROF-03

The system shall offer two interchangeable ways to set location: GPS auto-detect, or a manual region → city picker.

Must
FR-PROF-04

If location permission is denied or GPS fails, the system shall fall back to the manual picker without loss of progress.

Must
FR-PROF-05

GPS coordinates shall be used in memory only to derive a city name, and shall never be written to the database.

Must
FR-PROF-06

Skills shall be chosen from the fixed catalogue of 12 services (cleaning, cooking, laundry, childcare, elderly care, pet care, grocery, plant care, driving, tutoring, medical assist, babysitting).

Must
FR-PROF-07

A selected profile photo shall be compressed and stored inline on the user record.

Must
FR-PROF-08

The system shall mark the profile complete only when all required steps are satisfied, and shall resume an incomplete setup at the correct step.

Must
FR-PROF-09

Users shall be able to edit their own profile after setup.

Should
FR-PROF-10

The system shall never request or store street address, barangay, ZIP, phone number, or identity documents.

Must

Must

Must

Should

Should

The system shall support one-to-one text conversations between an employer and a helper.

Must

Must

Must
FR-MSG-06

Must
FR-MSG-07

Must

Must
FR-MSG-10

Must

2.8

Safety: reporting and blocking

Prefix FR-SAF

FR-SAF-01

The system shall offer Report and Block from within every conversation and from every profile detail view.

Must
FR-SAF-02

User reports shall accept one of: Inappropriate messages, Scam or fraud, Harassment or abuse, Fake profile, Other.

Must
FR-SAF-03

Selecting “Other” shall allow free-text entry of the reason.

Should
FR-SAF-04

Post reports shall accept one of: Scam or fraud, Inappropriate content, Spam or misleading, Harassment or abuse, Other.

Must
FR-SAF-05

A submitted report shall be acknowledged in the UI, and a repeat report of the same target shall be indicated as already reported.

Should
FR-SAF-06

Reports shall be write-only from the client: a client shall be able to create a report but never read the reports of others.

Must
FR-SAF-07

Blocking a user shall hide that user's listings and posts from the blocker, and shall prevent further messaging between the two parties.

Must
FR-SAF-08

The system shall provide a list of blocked users with the ability to unblock.

Must
FR-SAF-09

A post that reaches 3 reports shall be hidden automatically.

Should
FR-SAF-10

Report notifications sent to the operator shall carry identifiers only, never message content or personal details.

Must

Job posts and applications

Prefix FR-JOB

FR-JOB-01

Employers shall be able to publish job orders; helpers shall be able to publish “looking for work” notices.

Must
FR-JOB-02

The board shall provide tabs for All, Urgent, and Mine, and sorting by newest or oldest.

Must
FR-JOB-03

Posts shall be paginated at 10 per page.

Must
FR-JOB-04

A user shall be limited to 1 urgent and 2 regular posts per calendar day.

Must
FR-JOB-05

Remaining quota shall be displayed on the compose screen before submission.

Should
FR-JOB-06

Urgent posts shall expire 24 hours after creation; regular posts after 7 days. Expired posts shall not appear on the board.

Must
FR-JOB-07

The system shall reject post text and application pitches that appear to contain a phone number, an email address, or a long digit run.

Must
FR-JOB-08

A user shall be able to apply to a post with a short pitch, and shall be able to apply only once per post.

Must
FR-JOB-09

The post author shall see the applicant list, each row showing the applicant's city and how long ago they applied.

Must
FR-JOB-10

The applicant list shall load at most 30 applications per post.

Should
FR-JOB-11

The author shall be able to accept an application, which opens a conversation with that applicant, or reject it.

Must
FR-JOB-12

Rejection shall require confirmation before it is applied.

Should
FR-JOB-13

An applicant shall be notified when their application is accepted or rejected.

Must
FR-JOB-14

The author shall be able to remove their own post.

Must
FR-JOB-15

Application status shall be re-fetched when the screen regains focus, so a stale status is not displayed.

Prefix FR-ACC

FR-ACC-01

The system shall provide a Privacy & Security screen listing what is collected and the controls available.

Must
FR-ACC-02

The user shall be able to delete their account from within the app.

Must

Must
FR-ACC-10

Data and non-functional

What is stored

CollectionHolds
usersName, email, photo, role, city, terms acceptance, push tokens
helpersPublic listing: skills, rate, availability, schedule, bio, rating, review count
conversationsThreads, unread counts, last-read timestamps, block list
messagesText and sender only
reviewsOne per employer–helper pair, enforced by document id
posts / applicationsJob board, expiry timestamps, quota counters, pitches
reportsClient-create only, never client-readable
notificationsIn-app feed, server-written
appConfigVersion/maintenance gate and legal text
Data prohibition — DR-1

GPS coordinates, street address, barangay, ZIP, phone number, and identity documents shall never be persisted. Location fields hold city, region, and country names only.

Non-functional

IDRequirement
NFR-01Every remote call shall be wrapped in error handling and shall surface a loading state and an error state.
NFR-02No query shall fetch an unbounded collection; all lists shall be paginated or explicitly limited.
NFR-03Security rules shall enforce access server-side; the client shall never be the only check.
NFR-04The app shall degrade gracefully without network access — an error, not a crash or an indefinite hang.
NFR-05Crashes shall be captured with stack traces in release builds, subject to the user's opt-out.
NFR-06The app shall be usable on a small phone screen in portrait, controls reachable one-handed.
NFR-07Secrets shall not be committed to version control.

Out of scope for V1

  • Apple/iOS build and Apple Sign-In
  • In-app hiring workflow (mark-as-hired, availability blocking) and role switching
  • Countries other than the Philippines; full-text search
  • In-app voice calls; daily digest job alerts; image messages
  • Payments of any kind

How to cite this in a defect report

Name the requirement the behaviour violates. A report that does can be triaged without reproduction guesswork; one that does not usually cannot.

Example

FR-JOB-06 — FAIL. Urgent post created 2026-08-27 09:00 was still visible on the All tab at 2026-08-28 11:00 (26 h). Redmi Note 12, Android 14. Screenshot attached.
