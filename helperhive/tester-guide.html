Tester Guide

HelperHive connects employers who need household help — cleaning, cooking, childcare — with helpers looking for that work, anywhere in the Philippines. Browse, chat, arrange work directly. No fees, no commission.

Before it can go public, Google requires 12 testers opted in for 14 days straight. That's the main thing we need from you — plus any bugs you spot along the way.

Your time 15 minutes Try it once, then just leave it installed. No daily obligation.
You'll need Android + Google Android 7.0 or newer, and the Google account your phone uses for the Play Store.
The one rule Stay in 14 days If we drop below 12 testers, the counter resets to zero — for everyone.
1

How to join

  1. Send us the Google email your phone uses. It has to be the same account signed into the Play Store on that phone — the invite is tied to that address.
  2. Open the opt-in link on the phone, not on a computer.
  3. Tap Become a tester.
  4. On that same page, tap Download it on Google Play and install.
Your opt-in link — to be pasted here before this guide goes out —
Install from Play only

If someone hands you an APK file, installing that does not count toward the 12 testers. It has to arrive through the Play Store link.

After you accept, the app can take a few hours to appear on Play. If the store says "item not found", wait a bit and open the link again.

2

Your first run

  1. Welcome slides — swipe through.
  2. Sign in with Google. There's no "Continue with Facebook" yet; Google is the only way in for now.
  3. Accept the Terms & Privacy Policy. Worth a skim — it's short.
  4. Pick your role: "I need a helper" (Employer) or "I'm looking for work" (Helper). You can't switch later, so to see both sides you'd need a second Google account on a second phone.
  5. Set up your profile.
    • Employers: name and city. One step.
    • Helpers: photo, city, skills, schedule, short bio. Five quick steps.
  6. Tap Allow when Android asks about notifications. New messages and job applications arrive that way, and Android only offers that prompt twice before locking it out for good.

Posting limits, on purpose

You get 1 urgent + 2 regular posts a day. Urgent posts vanish after a day, regular ones after a week. When you run out, the app offers to unlock one more if you watch a short video (up to 2 extra a day) — please test that too. It's how the app is meant to pay for itself.

4

Not bugs

These are deliberate. No need to report them:

  • The ads are fake. The banners show Google's test ads during closed testing. Tapping one does nothing useful, and it's completely safe — it earns nothing and costs nothing.
  • There's no Facebook login. Hidden for now. Google sign-in only.
  • There's no iPhone version. Android only for this release.
  • You can't switch between Employer and Helper on one account.
  • It'll feel empty at first. The only profiles and job posts are the ones testers create. Posting something helps everyone else test.
5

Your privacy

The app was built to collect as little as it possibly can:

  • We store only your name, email and profile photo — all from your Google sign-in — plus your city.
  • We never store GPS coordinates, a street address, barangay, ZIP, phone number or any ID. If you let the app use your location, it works out the city name in the moment and throws the rest away. You can skip it entirely and pick your city from a list.
  • Other testers only ever see your name, photo, city, and whatever you write in your profile and posts.
  • You can delete your account and everything on it at any time, from Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Account. It's immediate and permanent. (Just not before day 14, please.)
Test data

This build runs against a separate test database that gets wiped before the public launch. Don't post anything you'd mind losing.

Found a bug? Tell us

Email helperhivesupport@gmail.com, or just message Sven directly — whichever is easier.

The more of this you can include, the faster it gets fixed:

  1. Which phone you have, and which Android version (Settings → About phone)
  2. What you did, step by step, right before it went wrong
  3. What you expected, versus what actually happened
  4. A screenshot or screen recording — single most useful thing you can send

Small stuff counts: a button that's hard to reach, wording that confused you, a screen that felt slow. Honest reactions beat polite ones — you can't hurt our feelings here.

7

If something goes wrong

What you're seeingWhat to try
"Item not found" on the Play Store You haven't accepted the opt-in link yet, or you accepted it with a different Google account than the one on your phone. Open the link again and check which account is signed in.
The app shows up but won't install An older copy may already be on the phone — including inside Second space, Dual apps or a work profile on Xiaomi, Samsung and similar. Uninstall it everywhere, then install from Play.
Google sign-in fails or spins forever Check you installed from Play rather than a sideloaded APK file. If it still fails, tell us which Google account you used.
No notifications arriving Settings → Apps → HelperHive → Notifications, and turn them on. Also check that battery saver isn't restricting the app.
"Update required" Update from Play — we may ship fixes during the test.
"We'll be right back" That's maintenance mode; we're fixing something on our end. Try again shortly.

The 14 days

Day 0 You accept the opt-in link and install from Play. This is when your clock starts.
Days 1–13 Keep it installed. Use it whenever you feel like it — there's nothing you have to do daily.
Day 14 We apply to Google for public launch.
The one thing that breaks it

Opting out or uninstalling early. If the count drops under 12, the clock restarts from zero for everybody. If you decide you're out, tell us before you leave so we can line up a replacement.
